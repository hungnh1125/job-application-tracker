Application strategy

Apply to both internships and full-time roles starting January 2027 or later. Prioritize roles suitable for software engineering experience plus PhD research.

  1. Find opportunities on Handshake, company sites, LinkedIn, Wellfound and YC.
  2. Record each opportunity as Saved first, including the original link, eligibility and deadline.
  3. Review CPT/OPT, sponsorship and graduation requirements before applying.
  4. Apply on the employer site; then update the stage to Applied and record the date.
  5. Set a follow-up date; update screening, interviews and offers.
  6. Export a backup weekly.
